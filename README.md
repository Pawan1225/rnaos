# RNA Optimization System

## AI + Quantum + HPC Platform for RNA Secondary Structure Optimization

RNAOS is a research platform exploring the intersection of:

~~~text
Artificial Intelligence

        +

Quantum Optimization

        +

High Performance Computing

        +

Computational Biology
~~~

The goal of RNAOS is to develop scalable computational methods for RNA secondary structure prediction and optimization, with a long-term vision toward AI-assisted quantum computing workflows for mRNA design.

---

# Project Motivation

RNA secondary structure plays an important role in mRNA behavior.

RNA folding influences:

- molecular stability
- translation efficiency
- structural behavior
- manufacturability of RNA-based therapeutics

However, RNA folding is a challenging computational problem because the number of possible structures grows rapidly with sequence length.

RNAOS investigates optimization-based approaches to explore this complex search space.
