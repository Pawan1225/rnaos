# ADR-001 — Why RNAOS

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-20

---

# Context

RNA secondary structure prediction involves complex combinatorial optimization problems. Existing tools often focus on isolated algorithms without providing an integrated platform for experimentation, optimization, explainability, and continuous learning.

---

# Decision

RNAOS will be developed as a Scientific Research Operating System that integrates:

- Classical optimization
- AI-driven decision making
- Quantum optimization
- Experiment management
- Knowledge accumulation
- Explainable optimization

---

# Rationale

A unified platform enables:

- Reproducible research
- Modular experimentation
- Continuous improvement
- Solver interoperability
- Long-term extensibility

---

# Consequences

Positive

- Unified architecture
- Reduced duplication
- Better research reproducibility
- Easier collaboration

Negative

- Higher initial engineering effort
- More architectural complexity
