# ADR-004 — Why PennyLane

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-20

---

# Context

RNAOS integrates quantum computing into optimization workflows and requires a framework that supports hybrid classical-quantum algorithms.

---

# Decision

PennyLane will serve as the primary quantum machine learning framework.

---

# Rationale

Advantages:

- Hybrid optimization
- Automatic differentiation
- Hardware agnostic
- Integration with PyTorch and JAX
- Strong research community

---

# Consequences

Positive

- Flexible quantum experimentation
- Easy integration with AI pipelines

Negative

- Rapid ecosystem evolution may require updates
