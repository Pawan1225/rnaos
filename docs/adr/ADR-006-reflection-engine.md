# ADR-006 — Why Reflection Engine

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-20

---

# Context

Scientific progress depends on learning from previous experiments rather than repeating identical approaches.

---

# Decision

RNAOS will include a Reflection Engine.

---

# Rationale

The Reflection Engine will:

- Analyze completed experiments
- Identify strengths and weaknesses
- Generate recommendations
- Improve future optimization strategies
- Support continuous learning

---

# Consequences

Positive

- Better experimentation
- Reduced repeated failures
- Improved optimization quality

Negative

- Additional storage and computational requirements
