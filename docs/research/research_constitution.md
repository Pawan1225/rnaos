# RNAOS Research Constitution

Version: 1.0

Status: Locked

---

# Vision

RNAOS is an Adaptive AI-Orchestrated Scientific Research Platform for RNA Secondary Structure Optimization using Classical, Quantum, and Quantum-Inspired Computing.

The objective is not only to predict RNA secondary structures but to build an intelligent scientific system capable of understanding, optimizing, evaluating, explaining, and continuously improving optimization strategies.

---

# Mission

Develop a production-grade research platform that combines Artificial Intelligence, Optimization, and Quantum Computing to advance RNA secondary structure prediction while maintaining reproducibility, explainability, and scientific rigor.

---

# Research Goals

1. Improve RNA secondary structure optimization.

2. Reduce optimization complexity.

3. Improve search-space efficiency.

4. Build adaptive optimization intelligence.

5. Integrate quantum and classical optimization.

6. Create reproducible scientific experiments.

7. Generate explainable optimization decisions.

8. Learn from previous optimization experience.
