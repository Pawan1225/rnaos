# References

## RNA Biology and Secondary Structure

1. Turner, D. H., & Mathews, D. H. (2010).

   "NNDB: The Nearest Neighbor Database for Predicting RNA Secondary Structure."

   RNA secondary structure prediction relies on thermodynamic models describing base pairing interactions and folding stability.

2. Mathews, D. H. et al. (2004).

   "Incorporating chemical modification constraints into a dynamic programming algorithm for prediction of RNA secondary structure."

   Journal of Molecular Biology.

---

## ViennaRNA Framework

3. Lorenz, R. et al. (2011).

   "ViennaRNA Package 2.0."

   Algorithms for Molecular Biology.

   Used as the classical Minimum Free Energy (MFE) RNA folding reference framework.

   Documentation:

   ViennaRNA Package

   https://www.tbi.univie.ac.at/RNA/

---

## RNA Folding and Optimization

4. Zuker, M. (1989).

   "On finding all suboptimal foldings of an RNA molecule."

   Science.

   Provides foundations for computational RNA folding and energy minimization approaches.

5. McCaskill, J. S. (1990).

   "The equilibrium partition function and base pair binding probabilities for RNA secondary structure."

   Biopolymers.

   Provides probabilistic approaches for RNA folding analysis.

---

## Quantum Optimization Methods

6. Farhi, E., Goldstone, J., & Gutmann, S. (2014).

   "A Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm."

   arXiv:1411.4028.

   Introduces the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) for solving combinatorial optimization problems.

7. Peruzzo, A. et al. (2014).

   "A variational eigenvalue solver on a photonic quantum processor."

   Nature Communications.

   Introduces Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) concepts.
